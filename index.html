02 · work

Flight software @ SpaceX

I write flight software for Starlink satellites at SpaceX. I live in Seattle and work in Redmond.

Starlink

Starlink satellite flight software

I work on star trackers, satellite cameras, tile cameras (which image Starship's heat shield), and other imaging products on the satellite bus. I've also worked on most of the other subsystems, including solar array actuators, propulsion, and power.
